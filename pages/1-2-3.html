Export Conditions (New BIS Framework)

To ship H200/MI325X to China, exporters must:
  1. Third-party lab testing to confirm chip capabilities
  2. No diversion of foundry capacity from US customers
  3. China/Macau shipments ≤ 50% of total US-bound quantity
  4. Case-by-case BIS review (not blanket approval)
