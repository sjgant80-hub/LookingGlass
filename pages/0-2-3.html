US Fiscal Trajectory

2026 deficit: ~$2.0T (6.5% of GDP)
Interest payments: $1.1T/yr — largest budget line
CBO projection: debt/GDP hits 150% by 2035
Refinancing wall: $9T in 2025-26 alone
10yr yield: 4.6% (up from 0.6% in 2020)

The math:
  If rates stay 4.5%+ and deficits stay 6%+:
  → Debt spiral becomes self-reinforcing by ~2028
  → Interest crowds out defense, infrastructure, social
  → Dollar confidence erodes not from rivals but from math

Three exits:
  1. Inflate away (financial repression) — most likely
  2. Austerity (politically impossible)
  3. Growth miracle (AI productivity?) — uncertain
