Three Exits from US Debt Spiral

1. Financial repression (inflate away) — most likely
   Negative real rates, slow erosion of purchasing power.
   Winners: debtors, asset holders. Losers: savers, fixed income.

2. Austerity — politically impossible
   Would require entitlement reform no party will touch.
   Trade war + defense spending make cuts harder.

3. Growth miracle (AI productivity?) — uncertain
   If AI delivers +1.5% GDP growth → math works.
   But tariffs + trade war dragging growth to 2.2%.
   Manufacturing at 9.4% of GDP, still shrinking.
