Climate Trajectory

Current path: +2.5-2.8°C by 2100 (vs 1.5° Paris target)
CO2: 425ppm (up from 280 pre-industrial)
Tipping points approaching:
  → Amazon dieback: possible at +2°C
  → Greenland ice sheet: committed at +1.5°C (passed)
  → Permafrost thaw: accelerating. CH4 feedback loop.
  → AMOC (Gulf Stream): weakening 15% since 1950

Physical impacts already locked in:
  → Sea level: +0.5m by 2050 regardless of emissions
  → Extreme heat: 3.5B people in "unsurvivable" zones by 2070
  → Water stress: 40% of global population by 2030
  → Crop yield: -10% per degree in tropics
