The Inflection

  1989 was PEAK κ for the Western system.

  The Cold War ended. Democracy won. Markets won.
  Nuclear war risk plummeted. 100+ million people freed.

  But three things happened that night at the Berlin Wall:

  1. THE UNIPOLAR ILLUSION
     No rival = no discipline. US could do anything.
     This led to: Iraq (2003), Afghanistan (2001-2021),
     financial deregulation, China WTO admission (2001).
     Every overreach of the next 30 years came from believing
     "we won" meant "we can't lose."

  2. THE CHINA MISCALCULATION
     Tiananmen taught the CCP: reform economy, lock politics.
     The West bet that trade would liberalize China.
     It didn't. It made China rich AND authoritarian.
     The biggest strategic error since Versailles.

  3. THE JAPAN WARNING (IGNORED)
     Asset bubbles end. 34 years of proof.
     But nobody applied the lesson to US housing (2008),
     or US tech stocks (2000), or US everything (2021).
     Japan was the canary. Nobody listened.
