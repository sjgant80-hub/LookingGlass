Contagion from Failure

Failed states don't just collapse inward. They export:
  → Refugees (8.8M from Sudan alone)
  → Jihadist safe havens (IS, JNIM, Al-Shabaab)
  → Weapons flows (Libya → Sahel)
  → Disease (cholera, polio reemergence)
  → Drug trafficking (Afghanistan→Iran→Europe)
  → Wagner/PMC exploitation (gold, uranium, cobalt)
