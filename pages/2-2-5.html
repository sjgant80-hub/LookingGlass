Model Strengths

1. STRUCTURAL DECLINE: excellent.
   Soviet collapse, Eurozone crisis, China property,
   Russia trajectory — all flagged by entropy rules.
   κ < 0.30 declining = terminal spiral.
   Track record: 5/5 on structural decline detection.

2. CONTAGION SPREAD: good.
   Asian crisis, Eurozone crisis, Arab Spring —
   connectivity multiplier correctly identifies transmission.
   Track record: 3/3 on spread pattern.

3. INVARIANTS: strong but untested at scale.
   Gold ≥ 0.65 holds for 5,000 years.
   India demographics: locked upward since 2005.
   Oil terminal: physics says yes. Market hasn't priced it.

Model Weaknesses

1. BLACK SWANS: cannot predict.
   COVID, volcanic eruptions, assassinations, pandemics.
   The model assumes continuity of current forces.
   Exogenous shocks break all projections.

2. TIMING: poor.
   The model sees WHAT but not WHEN.
   "Russia will collapse" is structural.
   "Russia will collapse in 2031" is a guess.
   All year-specific projections carry ±2yr uncertainty.

3. POLITICAL DISCONTINUITY: limited.
   Coups, revolutions, surprise elections.
   The Deciders page acknowledges 8 people fork timelines.
   But: the model can't predict what they'll decide.
   It can only model consequences of each fork.

4. FEEDBACK LOOPS: underspecified.
   AI displacement → unemployment → political instability
     → populism → bad policy → worse κ
   These chains are described but not quantified.
   Second-order effects may be larger than first-order.

5. CONNECTIVITY CHANGES: static.
   The model treats connectivity as fixed.
   But: trade war is actively changing connectivity.
   Bifurcation from 68% to 54% cross-bloc trade
   means 2020 connectivity weights are wrong in 2030.

Confidence Assessment

HIGH CONFIDENCE (retroactive + structural):
  Entropy rules, contagion spread, demographic lock,
  invariants (Gold, India upward, China downward, Oil decline)

MEDIUM CONFIDENCE (directional but imprecise):
  Country κ trajectories (±0.05 margin)
  Cascade chains (correct pattern, uncertain magnitude)
  Decade projections (right direction, ±2yr timing)

LOW CONFIDENCE (acknowledged unknowns):
  Fork outcomes (binary — model shows both paths)
  Black swan timing
  AI impact magnitude
  Second-order feedback loops

The model is a compass, not a clock.
It tells you WHICH WAY, not EXACTLY WHEN.
