The Inflection

  August 15, 1971 is the most important economic date since 1945.

  Before: Money = gold. Currencies = fixed. Deficits = constrained.
  After:  Money = trust. Currencies = floating. Deficits = infinite.

  Everything that followed — the inflation of the '70s,
  the financialization of the '80s, the debt explosion of the '00s,
  the money-printing of 2020 — traces back to this moment.

  The gold window didn't just close.
  It eliminated the last physical constraint on monetary expansion.

  US Debt/GDP trajectory:
    1971: 35%
    1981: 31% (Volcker briefly constrained)
    1991: 61%
    2001: 55% (brief Clinton surplus)
    2008: 68%

  Gold price trajectory:
