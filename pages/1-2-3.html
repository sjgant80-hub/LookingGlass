Export Control Architecture

US → China: H100/H200 banned. A100 banned. B200 banned.
  Workaround: Huawei Ascend 910B (~70% H100 perf)
  Workaround: Chip smuggling via SE Asia, Middle East
  Workaround: Stockpiled 2yr supply pre-ban

US → allies: ASML EUV to China banned (NL agreed)
  Japan: Tokyo Electron, Screen Holdings restricted
  Result: China locked out of <7nm for now
  But: SMIC 7nm exists (low yield, high cost)

China retaliation:
  Gallium, germanium export controls (Aug 2023)
  Rare earth processing: 70% global share
  Antimony, tungsten restrictions
  Message: "you need us for the supply chain too"

AI Economic Impact Projection

2026-2030: Augmentation phase
  → White-collar productivity +15-25%
  → Coding, legal, finance, customer service transformed
  → Job displacement: 5-10% of roles automated
  → New roles created: ~60% of displaced (net negative)

2030-2036: Displacement phase
  → Autonomous systems: driving, warehouse, manufacturing
  → AGI timeline debate: 2028-2035 range
  → If AGI by 2030: all projections break
  → Energy demand for AI: +500TWh by 2030 (= France)
  → Water demand for cooling: emerging constraint

κ impact: AI is the wildcard in every far-decade projection
  Best case: +0.05 global κ (productivity miracle)
  Base case: +0.01 (gains offset by disruption)
  Worst case: -0.03 (unemployment wave + concentration)
