Why This Breaks the Oil Invariant

Endgame [2,2,2] says: Oil ≤ 0.30 by 2036 (physics of transition)
Climate [2,2,3] says: 800GW solar+wind in 2025, 4.5TW pipeline

But the transition REQUIRES:
  → 2x current copper supply by 2035
  → 3x lithium supply by 2030
  → 2x rare earth supply by 2030
  → 2x cobalt supply by 2030

Current mining investment: 50% of what's needed.
Permitting timeline for new mines: 7-15 years.
Processing capacity: China controls 65-95% of everything.

If China restricts exports (as it has with gallium/germanium):
  → EV production halts outside China
  → Wind turbine production halts outside China
  → Grid buildout halts
  → Oil demand doesn't decline → Oil invariant breaks
  → Climate transition stalls at 40-50% not 80%+

China's Mineral Weapon

Aug 2023: Gallium + germanium export controls
Dec 2023: Graphite export controls
Oct 2024: Antimony + tungsten restrictions
Jan 2025: Rare earth processing technology export ban

Message: "You need our supply chain too."

This is the mineral mirror of the chip war:
  US controls: EUV machines, advanced chip design
  China controls: the minerals to build everything else
  Mutual destruction if either escalates to full embargo.

Trade War [1,1,3] bifurcation model assumes supply chains rewire.
But mineral processing takes 5-10yr to build.
China's 90% rare earth share took 30 years to establish.
There is no "friendshoring" for mineral processing at scale.
