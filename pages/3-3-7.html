The Seven Laws of Entropy History

  1. EVERY FIX CREATES THE NEXT CRISIS
     Gold window → inflation → Volcker → deregulation → dot-com
     → rate cuts → housing → QE → inequality → populism → tariffs

  2. EACH PEAK IS LOWER
     Reported κ peaks: 0.72 (1969) → 0.72 (1989) → 0.60 (2001)
     → 0.55 (2008) → 0.52 (2025). The system DEGRADES.

  3. THE NARRATIVE GAP IS CONSTANT (~0.06-0.15)
     Reported reality is ALWAYS more optimistic than actual reality.
     Only exception: 2020 (COVID too visible to spin).

  4. INTERVALS ARE SHORTENING
     1969→1979: 10 years between crises
     1989→2001: 12 years
     2001→2008: 7 years
     2008→2020: 12 years
     2020→2022: 2 years
     The system has less time to recover between shocks.

  5. EACH INTERVENTION IS LARGER
     Nixon's price freeze → Volcker's 20% rates → TARP $700B
     → QE $4.5T → COVID $5.9T → ??? ($10T+?)

  6. THE TRUTH IS ALWAYS VISIBLE IN HINDSIGHT
     Gold drain visible 1966-1969 → crash 1971
     OPEC leverage visible 1970-1973 → embargo 1973
     Japan bubble visible 1987-1989 → crash 1990
     Subprime visible 2005-2007 → crash 2008
     COVID debt visible 2020-2021 → inflation 2022

  7. NOBODY ACTS ON THE TRUTH UNTIL AFTER THE CRASH
     Because in a system where narrative diverges from reality,
     the truth-tellers are punished BEFORE the crash
     and vindicated AFTER — when it no longer matters.
