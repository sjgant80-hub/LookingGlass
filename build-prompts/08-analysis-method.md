# Frumkin Analysis Methodology

## κ Computation
κ = order / (order + entropy)
Equilibrium at φ⁻¹ ≈ 0.618

## Order Factors (0-1)
- GDP growth health (sustainable range)
- Employment health (inverse unemployment)
- Monetary policy appropriateness
- Institutional stability
- Currency stability

## Entropy Factors (0-1)
- Inflation deviation from 2% target
- Debt trajectory (rising = entropy)
- Currency depreciation rate
- Market volatility / external shocks

## Classification
- κ < 0.20: Terminal
- κ < 0.40: Critical
- κ < 0.55: Unstable
- κ < 0.65: Stable
- κ ≥ 0.65: Robust
