The Inflection

  2008 was the death of three beliefs:

  1. "MARKETS ARE EFFICIENT"
     The EMH crowd watched $11T vanish on a model error.
     Rating agencies gave AAA to junk. Banks levered 30:1.
     Nobody priced risk correctly. Nobody.
     Lesson learned? Dodd-Frank passed. Then weakened. Then...

  2. "GOVERNMENT SHOULDN'T INTERVENE"
     Free market ideology died on Sep 15, 2008.
     Every government on Earth intervened.
     TARP, QE, ZIRP, bank nationalizations.
     The question was never "should we?" but "how much?"

  3. "THE DOLLAR SYSTEM WORKS"
     It worked — barely — because the Fed could print.
     But every BRICS+ discussion, every gold purchase,
     every bilateral swap agreement traces to the moment
     the world watched the US system nearly implode.
